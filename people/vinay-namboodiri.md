Before joining Bath, Vinay has worked at the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Kanpur, Bell Labs Antwerp. Prior to that, he has been a Postdoctoral Fellow in KU Leuven with Prof. Luc Van Gool and obtained a Ph.D. while being guided by Prof. Subhasis Chaudhuri.

He is interested in research in Computer Vision and Machine Learning, specifically Multi-modal Human Centred AI, Generative AI and Beyond Supervised Learning.
