Libo is a Mathematics PhD student at the University of Bath. Her research spans generative modelling and computer vision, with a particular focus on diffusion models. She is broadly interested in developing principled generative methods for conditional generation and applications in visual and scientific domains. Her recent work explores both continuous and discrete diffusion models across computer vision and scientific machine learning.
