Yuchen's research lies at the intersection of Computer Graphics, Computer Vision, and Non-Photorealistic Rendering. He is interested in how artistic marks and brushstrokes can be analysed, represented, and reused for controllable image stylisation. His current work focuses on a geometry-aware vector representation of brushstrokes, combining trajectory and cross-sectional structure to capture the appearance of hand-drawn "painterly" strokes. More broadly, he is interested in style transfer and generative models for artistic media, especially approaches that connect low-level primitive representations, such as strokes, with high-level visual style.
