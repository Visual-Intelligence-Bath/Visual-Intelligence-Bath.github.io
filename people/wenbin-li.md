Prior to being an academic scholar, Wenbin was a Research Associate at Imperial SRL 2016-18 and UCL PRISM 2014-16 respectively. He also got his PhD at Bath MTRC 2013, MSc at Imperial 2009, as well as B.Eng at Xidian University 2008. Wenbin’s research interests lie in autonomous systems and their applications in manufacturing and professional capture, including the topics in multi-sensory based localisation and mapping, dynamic motion capture, as well as uncontrolled scene understanding and fabrication. Those topics are broadly relevant to fields of Robotics, Computer Vision, Graphics and Machine Learning.
